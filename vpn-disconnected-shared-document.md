# Incident: Remote Worker Unable to Access Shared Document

**Platform:** ServiceDesk Simulator
**Severity:** Medium
**Status:** Resolved

## Summary
A remote worker could not open a shared document needed for work.

## Troubleshooting and Resolution
1. Connected remotely to the worker's client machine.
2. Checked the connection and found the VPN was disconnected, which blocked access to company resources.
3. Reconnected the VPN.
4. Confirmed the worker could open the shared document.

## Outcome
Access was restored and the worker resumed work. Ticket closed.

## Takeaways
- Access problems for remote workers often come down to the VPN, so it's worth checking early.
- Confirming the symptom is fixed (opening the document) closes the loop.
